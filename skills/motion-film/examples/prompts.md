# Sample prompts

Copy one, fill the brackets, and send it to your agent with this skill installed. Each asks for the brief and directions first, so you approve before anything renders.

## Advert, 15 seconds

> Use the motion-film skill. Make a 15-second advert for [product], for [X and Instagram], sound off by default. One benefit: [the benefit]. Call to action: [visit url / join the waitlist]. Brand: logo at [path], font [name] at [path], colours [hex list]. Truth boundaries: [what is live, what is planned]. Masters in 16:9, 9:16 and 1:1. Start with the brief and two directions.

## Launch film, 45 seconds

> Use the motion-film skill to plan a 45-second launch film for [product]. Story: one person, [their situation], the problem [specific friction], how [product] changes it. No voiceover; on-screen text carries it. Study these references for pacing only: [paths or links]. Show me three directions with time tables, then stop for my choice.

## Educational explainer, 2 minutes

> Use the motion-film skill. Turn this article into a 2-minute explainer: [paste or path]. Audience: [who]. Numbered chapters, one running example with consistent illustrative figures, a recap at the end. I will record the voiceover myself: write the script for speech, then key the timeline to my recording's word timings. Captions burned in and as .srt.

## Product walkthrough

> Use the motion-film skill to make a 60-second walkthrough of [feature]. Use real captures from [url / app] for the three actions that need proof, and authored scenes for how the money or data moves. Label anything not live as "Planned".

## Motion study only

> Use the motion-film skill. Before the full film, build a 7-second motion study of the hardest transition: [describe it, e.g. a chat message becoming a signed agreement]. Final finish, with sound. I want to approve the look and easing first.

## Social cut from an existing master

> Use the motion-film skill. Re-compose the film in [path to source] for 9:16 and 1:1. Re-lay out each scene for the frame; keep labels out of the platform UI zones; do not centre-crop.

## Review and QC an existing video

> Use the motion-film skill's QC checklist on [path to mp4]. Run the measured checks, make a contact sheet and transition strips, and list every problem with its timestamp.
